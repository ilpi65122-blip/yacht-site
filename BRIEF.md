# Brief — Yacht site

> Written by the owner, signed off by the owner (see SIGNOFF.md). OneNote is scratch; this is the settled version.

## What the site is
A listings site where I publish yachts for people who want to **buy or rent** them.
Visitors browse the yachts and enquire about the ones they like.

## Pages / sections
- **Home** — intro + featured yachts.
- **Listings** — all yachts, with filters.
- **Single yacht page** — full details + photos for one yacht.
- **Contact** — how to get in touch / enquire.

## Look and feel
Clean, elegant, premium. **Navy and white.** Photo-led — big images, plenty of white space, calm.

## Must-haves
Each yacht listing shows:
- Photos
- Name
- Price
- Length / size
- Location
- Buy or rent
- Short description
- An **enquire** button (way to contact about that yacht)

Listings page has **filters**:
- By **buy or rent**
- By **price**

## Out of scope (for now)
Leave these for later builds:
- Online payments
- User accounts / login
- Booking calendar
- The CRM (separate build)

The first site just **shows yachts and lets people enquire**.
